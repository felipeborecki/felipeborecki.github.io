(2) Contact form: If you use the contact form, the data you enter (name, email address, message) will be stored for the purpose of processing your inquiry and in case of follow-up questions. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in responding to your inquiry).
