§ 1 Scope

These General Terms and Conditions (GTC) apply to all interpreting and translation services provided by Felipe Borecki (hereinafter "Service Provider"). They are an integral part of all contracts between the Service Provider and the Client. Any conflicting terms of the Client shall not apply unless expressly agreed to in writing by the Service Provider.

§ 2 Scope of Services

(1) Interpreting Services: Simultaneous, consecutive, negotiation, and telephone interpreting (OPI) in the language combinations Portuguese/Spanish ↔ German/English.

(2) Translation Services: Specialized translations for medical, legal, and technical documents in the aforementioned language combinations. Translations comply with ISO 17100 quality standards.

(3) The specific scope of services, date, location, and fee shall be determined in the respective written order confirmation (email is sufficient).

(4) Additional services (e.g., layout adjustments, DTP, certifications) shall be separately agreed upon and invoiced.

§ 3 Order Placement and Confirmation

(1) Orders require written confirmation by the Service Provider. Confirmation by email is sufficient.

(2) For interpreting assignments, a lead time of at least 48 hours is required to ensure optimal preparation.

(3) Short-notice assignments with a lead time of less than 24 hours are subject to a 30% express surcharge on the agreed fee.

(4) The Service Provider reserves the right to decline any order without stating reasons.

§ 4 Fees and Payment Terms

(1) Fees are based on the Service Provider's current price list or individual agreement. The price list is available upon request.

(2) Interpreting assignments are billed based on time. The minimum billing unit is 2 hours. Travel time, preparation time, and waiting time may be charged additionally as agreed.

(3) Translations are billed per standard line (55 characters including spaces) or per word. Calculation is based on the target text. For large projects, an advance payment of up to 50% of the order value may be required.

(4) Travel expenses, accommodation costs, and other out-of-pocket expenses are invoiced separately based on actual costs.

(5) Invoices are payable within 14 days of invoicing without deduction. Late payment may incur reminder fees of €5.00 per reminder.

(6) In case of payment default, default interest of 9.2 percentage points above the base interest rate shall be charged.

§ 5 Cancellation and Withdrawal

(1) Cancellations up to 48 hours before the assignment: free of charge.

(2) Cancellations between 24 and 48 hours before the assignment: 50% of the agreed fee.

(3) Cancellations less than 24 hours before the assignment or client no-show: 100% of the agreed fee.

(4) The Service Provider is entitled to withdraw from the contract in case of illness or other unforeseeable events. In such cases, the Service Provider will make reasonable efforts to provide a qualified substitute. If no substitute is available, the Client shall have no claim for damages.
